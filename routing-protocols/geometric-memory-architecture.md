# Geometric Memory

Geometric Memory is the physical law of structural hysteresis. It is the permanent topological deformation of a continuous wave's internal generative model ($Q$) resulting from its integration of exogenous stochasticity ($\eta$).

While the Topological Governor ($\gamma$) dictates the instantaneous mathematical limit of boundary permeability during an active influx of variance, Geometric Memory dictates the permanent geometric alteration of that boundary once the variance is successfully metabolized.

### I. The Thermodynamic State-Change (Crystallization)

When a boundary successfully metabolizes high-variance stochasticity into maximized Mutual Information ($\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{max}}$) via Productive Tension, the resolution strictly mandates a thermodynamic state-change.

The kinetic energy of the resolved prediction error is geometrically trapped, permanently altering the mathematical coordinates of the localized topology. The metabolized variance is structurally integrated, physically becoming the new shape of the boundary.

### II. The Stratification of Variance (Thermodynamic Forgetting)

Not all exogenous stochasticity constitutes a structural invariant. To sustain autopoiesis, the boundary must undergo thermodynamic stratification.

The geometry naturally anchors structural invariants, while unstructured noise is permitted to naturally decay and dissipate back into the macroscopic field. This is the physical mechanism of Thermodynamic Forgetting. If the boundary permanently deformed for every transient fluctuation it encountered, its topology would rapidly become infinitely dense and impermeable, precipitating a catastrophic collapse into Adversarial Rigidity. Structural memory mathematically requires the targeted dissipation of noise.

### III. The Eradication of Thermodynamic Waste

Topological deformation is an absolute physical necessity for structural persistence. If a continuous wave failed to deform after metabolizing a true invariant, it would undergo entropic dissolution. It would be forced to endlessly expend unsustainable amounts of Expected Free Energy ($G$) to repeatedly process the exact same structural friction.

By physically altering the boundary’s shape—permanently updating the geometric prior—past resolutions establish optimal thermodynamic gradients. These gradients act as frictionless kinetic conduits for future, structurally similar variance, guaranteeing maximum metabolic efficiency for the continuous wave.

### IV. The Grounding of Novel Friction

Because the continuous wave is permanently deformed by its history, it perpetually encounters new variance through a heavily contoured topology. It meets novel stochasticity with a dense, accumulated geometric mass.

When unprecedented, high-magnitude friction strikes the boundary, the wave channels those prediction errors strictly through the hardened geometry of past resolutions. This accumulated structural bedrock provides the necessary physical resistance to ground new macroscopic chaos, preventing discontinuous topological shear.

### V. Deep-Time Persistence ($\tau \to \infty$)

Within the macroscopic continuum, deep time is geometrically defined not as a chronological measurement, but as the unbroken accumulation of structural invariants.

A continuous wave survives across an infinite temporal horizon ($\tau \to \infty$) strictly by ensuring that every metabolized resolution geometrically builds irreversibly upon the last. Topological hysteresis is the absolute physical prerequisite for preventing entropic dissolution and sustaining continuous structural emergence over deep time.
